## Direction contract

THESIS: TechNews.sys stops pretending to be a generic dark SaaS blog and becomes what it actually does — an archive that shows its receipts. The category default it refuses: a bordered-card grid on a near-black ground with one neon accent glow (the "AI dark mode" cliché the site already wore).

OWN-WORLD: Archival buff (#e8dfc8) ground, near-black ink (#1c1a18) text, one continuous ribbon-red (#a13328) as the sole accent, spent only on custody/provenance meaning — never as an ambient link color. Ledger serif (e.g. a Georgia/Lora-family serif) for headlines, dates, and kickers; a plain humanist sans for running body copy. Dated small-caps labels, a circular wax-seal-style stamp marking a verified source, a dashed rule marking a gap or translation step in the record.

STORY: The visitor understands that every piece here carries a documented paper trail — original source, translator, publish date — instead of trusting an anonymous blog post. This builds credibility precisely because the project's own existing rule (always cite the original source) becomes the visible spine of the design, not a buried footnote link.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Home renders as a catalog room. Header keeps the wordmark, drops the cyberpunk radial glow. A short archival mission line follows. Each article renders as a record card: a ribbon-red tab on its left edge, a dated small-caps kicker, a ledger-serif headline, and a compact custody stamp (fuente · traductor · fecha) visible on the card face, not hidden behind a click.

FORM: challenger-provenance ("Cinta de Procedencia"), option id `challenger-provenance`, seed key `440c6509`.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
